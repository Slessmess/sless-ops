This notice explains how Sless Is More handles information on slessismore.org, including our merchandise store. It also explains your choices about analytics and advertising.

Contact and privacy requests

For questions about this notice or to request access, correction or deletion of your information, contact {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. Please do not email payment-card numbers or passwords. We may ask for information needed to verify a request before releasing or changing account or order information.

Information used to run the store

When you place an order or contact us, we process the information you provide, such as your name, email address, billing and delivery details, order items, payment status and messages. We use it to process payments, fulfill and support orders, communicate about your purchase, handle returns or disputes, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.

If you create an account, WordPress and WooCommerce store the account information needed to provide it. Security and hosting systems may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and access logs to operate the website, prevent abuse and diagnose problems.

Service providers

  • SiteGround, WordPress and WooCommerce: website hosting, security, store operation and order records.
  • Stripe: payment processing and fraud prevention. Payment-card entry is handled through the payment provider. We receive information needed to identify and manage the payment, such as its status and transaction reference.
  • Printify, the applicable print provider and delivery carriers: information needed to produce and deliver print-on-demand merchandise, including delivery details and order items.
  • Mailchimp: newsletter subscription information if you choose to subscribe. Marketing email is separate from order communications; you can unsubscribe using the link in marketing messages.
  • Google Analytics: optional website-usage measurement when you allow statistics cookies.
  • Google Ads and Meta: optional advertising measurement and remarketing when you allow marketing cookies.

Providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy policies. Their systems may process information outside your country. See SiteGround, Automattic, Stripe, Printify, Intuit/Mailchimp, Google and Meta for more information.

Cookies and your choices

Necessary cookies support the shopping cart, checkout, account sessions, security and remembering your privacy choices. These functions remain available if you reject optional cookies.

Statistics: Google Analytics can measure visits, pages viewed and interactions. These cookies are optional.

Marketing: Google Ads and Meta can measure visits and purchases, attribute advertising results, and build audiences to show relevant ads. Information may include page or product interactions, order value and currency, browser/device identifiers and advertising identifiers. Sharing identifiers for advertising can be treated as sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising under some privacy laws. Reject marketing cookies to opt out of this site's optional advertising tags.

Use Accept all, Reject optional, or the individual choices in the cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the Cookie settings control. Optional categories start off. A choice is not required to browse or buy merchandise. Your browser can also block or delete cookies; doing so may reset your choices or affect cart and login functions.
