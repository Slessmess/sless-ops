name: WordPress brand and launch boundaries
description: Owner constraints for preserving the current site's identity and activating the drafted storefront.
---

The owner values the existing homepage footage and feel. Improve its presentation,
SEO-friendly structure, and clarity for new visitors without replacing that footage.

**Why:** The owner explicitly asked to keep the current homepage footage and feel
while making the site more professional.

**How to apply:** Reuse original media references and real match content. A blocked
file is a hosting/access issue to flag and restore, not permission to invent or
substitute footage.

The WordPress refresh is draft-only until the owner authorizes launch. Draft creation
does not authorize switching the front page, changing the global theme, or publishing.
The owner separately authorized switching WooCommerce Stripe to test mode and staging
SlessBox checkout/downloads on 2026-10-08. They subsequently requested an end-to-end
test once setup is ready; this is not public-launch or unspecified real-cost authorization.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested drafts and exact placeholder destinations.

**How to apply:** Keep existing public page content unchanged during staging; distinguish
inactive placeholders from connected services in delivery summaries. Recheck actual
page statuses before writing: the older draft manifest does not establish that pages
are still drafts. If an original is public, create a separate staging draft.

The owner explicitly authorized opening the merchandise store and enabling live
WooCommerce payments by saying “turn it on” after being told both test mode and
Coming Soon were still enabled. This supersedes draft-only restrictions for
merchandise checkout, not for SlessBox or unrelated pages.

**Why:** The owner directly requested public merchandise sales.

**How to apply:** This WooCommerce launch authorization is historical. The owner
subsequently confirmed merchandise uses the Printify shop, not WooCommerce.
Keep the current Printify merchandise checkout; do not reactivate WooCommerce
merchandise checkout or propose it as a fix for Printify purchase buttons.
This does not authorize agent-initiated real charges, production orders,
unrelated publication, or opening SlessBox paid tiers.

The owner also authorized public homepage merchandise promotion and site-wide
navigation improvements: “lets put this on the front page” and “it's hard to find
all the merch items on the site.”

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested featuring the approved Halloween products
and making all merchandise easy for customers to find.

**How to apply:** Homepage merchandising and customer-navigation improvements are
no longer draft-only. Preserve the existing wrestling identity and footage; this
does not authorize unrelated offer changes or activating paid SlessBox tiers.

Use the owner's approved product names, prices, prizes, and entry mechanics without
silently correcting them. Flag inconsistent savings claims or historical dates for
owner review rather than inventing replacement offers or campaign dates.

**Why:** The owner explicitly said not to change names, prices, prizes, or entry rules.

**How to apply:** Get revised facts from the owner before changing those claims.

Use WooCommerce's native order-specific download permissions for staged paid SlessBox
delivery, not a custom login or a shared Stripe receipt password. This replaces the
earlier password-plugin plan for paid delivery. Page protection alone does not make a
publicly accessible installer URL private.

**Why:** Native order permissions connect file access to payment, whereas a shared
password does not. The owner had prohibited a custom login system; retaining the
existing WooCommerce platform avoids adding one.

**How to apply:** Use the protected product-file upload workflow and verify that direct
unauthorized file requests are denied before enabling delivery. Do not upload the
installer to the ordinary public Media Library or claim free-email download delivery,
license provisioning, or successful payment from static confirmation copy.
