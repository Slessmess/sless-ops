The business is named Sless Media Group. Start with restaurants, then expand to
other customer types.

**Why:** The user said “restaurants then we'll grow from there” and named the
business Sless Media Group.

**How to apply:** Prioritize restaurant workflows at launch without treating
restaurants as a permanent limit on the business.

Offer both self-service and owner-managed social-media marketing. Self-service
is the lower, cheaper tier; the owner-managed service is more expensive.

**Why:** The user said “do both. if they manage it themselves it will be a lower
tier and cheaper. If I do it it will be more expensive.”

**How to apply:** Include both customer paths rather than building only one.
The business should create engaging customer videos and posts to support leads
and sales.

The user approved starter packages: self-service at $99/month with 4 short
videos and 12 posts; owner-managed at $499/month with 8 short videos and 20 posts.
Billing remains inactive until generation costs and customer flows are verified.

**Why:** The user accepted these starter packages and the first-version scope;
this approval does not authorize collecting subscription payments yet.

**How to apply:** Preserve these package prices and monthly quantities when
preparing subscriptions. Treat preview allowances separately from paid
entitlements. Verify production costs and payment/customer flows before asking
the owner to authorize activation. Automatic social publishing requires the
restaurant's account connections; downloads alone do not count as publishing.
