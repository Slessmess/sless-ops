---
name: Merchandise price evidence
description: Evidence standards and pricing boundaries for the Sless Printify merchandise range.
---

Use only dated, current, single-item consumer retail offers as competitive benchmarks. Exclude wholesale or blank-garment prices, minimum-order pricing, sold-out listings, and quote-only services. Record the product match and whether shipping is included, separately stated, or unknown; do not claim delivered-price parity when shipping is unknown.

**Why:** The owner wants competitive prices without treating production-cost listings or incomplete checkout totals as like-for-like retail evidence.

**How to apply:** Target $2 below a verified comparable item or delivered offer only when the recorded Printify production cost plus the estimated payment-fee allowance permits it. Label the fee allowance as an estimate, not a verified fee or guaranteed margin. Apply this work to the 50 additional drafts only; preserve the original three shirts and do not publish or accept orders until the sales and fulfillment path is verified.
