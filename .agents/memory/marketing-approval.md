name: Marketing approval
description: Owner review is required before Sless advertising creatives are posted.
---

The owner said: “let me confirm all the ads and then lets post them.”
Show the actual image/video files and the proposed captions before publishing.
Do not interpret the earlier broad request to post a campaign as approval of
unreviewed creative.

**Why:** The owner explicitly requested confirmation before publication.

**How to apply:** Identify each ad clearly and obtain approval for the final
versions. Keep drafts and suggested posting dates separate from published posts
and active schedules. Reconfirm materially changed creative before posting.

The owner also requires pixels, Google ad tracking and the other retargeting setup
to be ready before posting, and a clickable website link in the Story.

**Why:** The owner said: “also include a link to the website in the story when you
post it. and before posting lets get pixels, google ad tracking and anything else
we need setup so we can retarget”.

**How to apply:** Do not substitute a printed URL for a clickable Story link.
Explain any native-app step required for adding the link sticker.

The owner subsequently chose to defer hosted-shop tracking fixes and proceed
with organic marketing; this does not authorize paid spend or establish
retargeting readiness.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose to leave the tracking IDs unchanged and
continue with marketing despite the unresolved hosted-shop event checks.

**How to apply:** Keep tracking marked deferred and unverified, not a blocker
for approved organic posts. Paid advertising still requires separate approval.

Stagger the campaign; do not publish all approved ads at once.

**Why:** The owner said: “Stager it out. Don't just post all of the ads at once”.

**How to apply:** Treat approval as permission for individual approved creatives,
not simultaneous publication. Preserve spacing between posts and the last-day
creative's October 25 restriction. Distinguish a saved plan from active scheduling.

Campaign publication includes Instagram and the Sless Taylor Facebook Page.

**Why:** The owner pointed out that the launch was on Instagram but missing
from the Sless Taylor Facebook Page.

**How to apply:** Verify a publication receipt on each platform separately.
Do not assume Instagram publishing cross-posts to Facebook.

The owner chose to keep the existing Facebook sale schedules and hold the
replacement organic campaign.

**Why:** The owner explicitly selected “Keep them; hold the replacement campaign”
after being informed that seven outdated sale posts remained scheduled.

**How to apply:** Do not cancel or revise those schedules, or publish the
replacement campaign on either platform, without new owner instructions.
